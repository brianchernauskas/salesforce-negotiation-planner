This tool provides strategic guidance based on publicly reported Salesforce contracting practices and procurement benchmarks. Salesforce does not publish renewal uplift or discount data — all ranges are directional estimates, not vendor-confirmed figures. Actual outcomes depend on account team priorities, timing within Salesforce's fiscal year, and your credible alternatives.
